THE CORKHAM · PLOT 15

Explore at your own pace.

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom and right-drag to pan. Select a floor, then click its rooms for details. Click a door leaf to open or close it.

Dimensions offers published room envelopes, inferred overall and opening sizes, and a two-point 3D measurement tool. Choose Metric, Imperial or Both. Click a dimension for its source status.

Walkthrough: WASD move, mouse look, E operates the door ahead, Shift walks faster and M toggles the map. Escape releases the mouse. Exit Walkthrough restores the previous presentation view.

Shortcuts: 1 House, 2 Ground, 3 First, D Dimensions, M Measure, F Furniture, R Reset.

Interactive reconstruction based on published marketing floor plans. Dimensions and layouts should be verified against plot-specific construction documents for technical use. All wall coordinates are inferred. Heights, roof and opening sizes are assumptions. Bedroom numbering is inferred.

Source: Taylor Wimpey, The Corkham, Plot 15, Colston Gardens. This independent viewer is not endorsed or produced by Taylor Wimpey.
